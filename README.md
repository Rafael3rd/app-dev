
# My Favorite Series: The Beginning After the End

**The Beginning After the End (TBATE)** is a fantasy manhwa/webtoon featuring action, adventure, magic, and reincarnation.

I personally prefer the **manhwa version** because I enjoy its artwork, storytelling, and character development. I'm not a fan of the anime adaptation, as I prefer how the original comic presents the story.

## About the Series

**The Beginning After the End (TBATE)** is a fantasy, action, and adventure series written by *TurtleMe*. It follows the story of Arthur Leywin, a former king who gets reincarnated into a magical world.

Unlike his previous life, Arthur gets another opportunity to experience childhood, build relationships, discover magic, and create a different future for himself.

> A second chance at life gives Arthur the opportunity to experience friendship, adventure, and a destiny different from his previous life.

## Main Character: Arthur Leywin

Arthur Leywin is the protagonist of the series. He was previously known as King Grey, a powerful king who lived a lonely life before being reincarnated.

Because of his previous experiences, Arthur possesses knowledge, maturity, and abilities that make him different from ordinary children in his new world.

## Why I Like Arthur Leywin

What makes Arthur my favorite character is that he doesn't follow the usual formula of many isekai protagonists.

Unlike many reincarnated main characters, Arthur doesn't use his new life as an excuse to pursue a harem or romanticize relationships with characters simply because they are in a younger body. He also doesn't constantly pretend to have the mentality of an ordinary child despite carrying the memories and experiences of his previous life.

What I appreciate most is his honesty. **Arthur eventually confesses his reincarnation to his parents**, allowing them to understand his true circumstances instead of keeping such an important part of himself a secret forever. This is something I rarely see other isekai protagonists do.

### What Makes Arthur Different?

- **Maturity:** He carries the mindset and experiences of his previous life rather than pretending to have the mentality of an ordinary child.
- **Honesty:** He eventually tells his parents about his reincarnation, showing how much he values his family and their relationship.
- **Character:** His motivations go beyond romance or building a harem.
- **Responsibility:** He takes his second life seriously and understands that his decisions affect the people around him.
- **Character Development:** He learns from his mistakes and continues growing throughout the story.

> What makes Arthur interesting isn't simply that he was reincarnated, but how he chooses to live his second life.

For me, Arthur is a refreshing change from the typical isekai protagonist. His personality, maturity, honesty, and relationship with his family are some of the biggest reasons I enjoy *The Beginning After the End*.

## Why I Like This Series

Aside from Arthur, I also enjoy the series because of:

1. Its interesting fantasy world and magic system.
2. Great character development.
3. Exciting battles and adventures.
4. Emotional moments and meaningful relationships.
5. A story that explores the consequences of having a second chance at life.
